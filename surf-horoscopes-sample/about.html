MADE BY A SURFER

A little closer
to the sea.

I'm Lachie Perris.

I'm a surfer and a wave forecaster with a PhD in coastal geoscience. I've spent years studying how waves travel, change and meet the coast. But I also know that surfing can never be captured in numbers.

I made Surf Horoscopes to bring that human feeling into a forecast. Waves, wind and tides give us a picture of the ocean; astrology offers another way to reflect on how we might meet it. Neither can tell us exactly what a session will mean to us.

My hope is that these readings help us feel more connected to the water, and empower us to feel the ocean in our own way.

HOW IT WORKS

The forecast is based on offshore model guidance and nearby tide predictions. The horoscope is an imaginative reflection, not a safety forecast. Check local conditions before you enter the water.

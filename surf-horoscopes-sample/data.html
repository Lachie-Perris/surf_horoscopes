HOW TO READ IT

Wave height describes offshore significant wave height, not breaking wave height at the beach. Direction arrows point where the waves and wind travel. Tide curves are interpolated between Bureau of Meteorology high and low predictions for Sydney (Fort Denison) and Brunswick Heads; they are guidance only, not for navigation.
